How it works
1

Upload any .txt publication unit file. Records are grouped by 8005 / 9010 markers.

2

Each 0016 address is parsed locally — covers European, American (full state names), Canadian (full province names), NZ, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Middle Eastern and other formats.

3

If any cities couldn't be extracted automatically, a review step lets you fill them in manually — or skip and fill after seeing the results.

4

A 0006 : City line is inserted after 0009 and before 0016. ³ characters, latin-1 encoding, and everything else stay untouched.
